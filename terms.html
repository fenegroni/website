3. How the contract is made

You pay through a checkout page run by Stripe, my payment provider. Stripe then emails you a receipt and an invoice. I never see or store your card details.

  • Build it together. You pay through the "Book an hour" button on this site. Our contract starts when Stripe confirms your payment.
  • Done for you. The quote request and my quote do not commit you to anything. Once you choose an option, I email you a payment link for it. Our contract starts when Stripe confirms that payment.

4. Booking and rescheduling

I will contact you within 2 business days of your payment. For an hour together, we agree the focus and a time for our video call. For a done-for-you option, we agree a time for the handover call. If I can't help with what you need, I refund you in full before we meet.

You can move any call free of charge if you tell me at least 24 hours before it starts. If I need to move the call, I will offer you a new time, and you keep the full service.

5. What we build

Every solution uses tools and accounts in your name. You pay for any running costs those tools need. If I build the solution for you, you give me only the access I need, and you can remove that access once I hand it over. What we build belongs to you. You can change it yourself, and you are responsible for how you use it after we finish.

Your hour includes a short written recap, which I send after the call. For seven days after the call, you can email me questions about what we built, at no extra cost.

Later help is new work. If something stops working or the problem itself changes, email me. I tell you before any extra help costs money. You can then book another hour, or I send you a fixed quote, and we agree it in the same way.

6. Your right to cancel

This section applies if you are buying as a consumer, meaning for purposes outside your trade, business or profession.

You have the right to cancel this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The cancellation period ends 14 days after the day we made the contract. To cancel, tell me clearly by email to hello@fenegroni.com. You may use the model form below, but you do not have to.

If you cancel, I will refund your payment within 14 days of the day you told me. I will use the same payment method you used, and you will not pay any fee for the refund.

When you pay, you ask me to start the work within the 14 days. If you then cancel, you must pay for the part of the work I have already done. I will work that amount out in proportion to the full price. Once I have fully provided the service at your request, you can no longer cancel it.

An hour together counts as fully provided when our video call ends. The written recap and the seven days of email questions come with it, but they do not keep the cancellation period open. A done-for-you option counts as fully provided when the handover call ends.

If you buy on behalf of a business, this cancellation right does not apply. You can still move the call under section 4.
