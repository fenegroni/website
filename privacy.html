Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information I collect when you contact me or buy a service through fenegroni.com, and how I use it. If you have any questions, email me at hello@fenegroni.com.

1. Who I am

I am Filippo Negroni, a sole trader trading as fenegroni.com. I am the controller of your personal information under UK data protection law. My address is Flat 47 Aviator House, Addlestone, KT15 2US.

2. What I collect

  • Your name and email address, when you email me or buy a service.
  • Your billing details, when you pay. Stripe collects these on its checkout page. I see your name, email, billing address and the last four digits of your card. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Anything you choose to tell me in emails or during sessions, such as details about your business.

My site has no contact forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies.

4. Who else receives it

I use a small number of trusted providers to run my business. Each one handles your information under its own privacy policy.

  • Stripe processes payments, receipts and invoices.
  • Google hosts my email through Gmail. The site also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like any web host, it records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security.

These providers may store data in the United States. They protect transfers out of the UK with safeguards that UK law recognises, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual clauses.

5. How long I keep it

  • I keep client records and invoices for 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, because UK tax law requires it.
  • I keep emails from people who do not become clients for up to 2 years after our last contact, and then I delete them.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask me to delete your information, unless the law requires me to keep it;
  • object to or ask me to limit how I use your information;
  • ask me to send your information to you or to another organisation in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email me at hello@fenegroni.com. I will reply within one month.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
