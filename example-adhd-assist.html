Example

When I get stuck

On a good day I know what helps me get going again. When I am stuck, I cannot reach that advice, even though it is my own.

What I built

I write my own rules while I feel well. The AI notices the signs that I am stuck and offers one small thing from those rules. It reads my own judgement back to me at the moment it is hardest to reach.

Why build it, when apps exist?

  • It is convenient. It lives inside the AI tool I already use all day, so there is no new app to open.
  • It removes friction. It speaks only when the signs appear, and it uses my words instead of a timer's.
  • It was quick to get done, because it builds on a tool I already had.
  • It fits my way of working, because I wrote the rules myself.

The code is public on GitHub.
