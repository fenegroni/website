What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a helper on your phone or computer that does a job for you, like answering emails, sorting paperwork or keeping track of bookings. You tell it what you need in plain words. I set one up with you and show you how it works as we go, so you can carry on by yourself.

  1. 1

    We choose one job that eats up your time, so you see a difference quickly.

  2. 2

    Set it up together

    We set up an AI helper to do that job, side by side and at your pace.

  3. 3

    Carry on by yourself

    You learn how it works, so you can hand the next job over without me.

Case study

Checking council figures

Three UK councils publish figures on stray dogs, each in their own format. I built an AI tool that gathered them into one tidy set of numbers that anyone can check. A separate checker, written in ordinary code, went over every figure. It found two adding-up mistakes in the councils' own published figures, and I confirmed both against the original documents.

For companies and charities

Working with a larger team?

I start with workshops where your team walks me through the work that matters most. I map out how it gets done today. Then I write a clear plan that your IT team can build from, covering what the AI helper should do, what it needs, and how you will know it is working. I understand the technology as well as the people who use it, so the plan stays realistic.

I aim for AI that helps people rather than replaces them. Your people keep making the judgement calls, and the AI takes on the repetitive work around them. A large company and a five-person charity start the same way. We begin with how you work, not with a product.

Workshops and planning

I run workshops with leaders to find where AI could save the most time, and then I see the work through. That covers deciding what to build, designing how the AI helper talks and works, choosing the tools, testing, launching, and improving it once it is live. At every stage I hand over plans your IT team can build from.
