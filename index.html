For companies and charities

Working with a larger team?

I start with a discovery session where your team walks me through the work that matters most. I map out how it gets done today. Then I write a clear plan that your IT team can build from, covering what the AI helper should do, what it needs, and how you will know it is working. I understand the technology as well as the people who use it, so the plan stays realistic.

I aim for AI that helps people rather than replaces them. Your people keep making the judgement calls, and the AI takes on the repetitive work around them. A large company and a five-person charity start the same way. We begin with how you work, not with a product.

Workshops and planning

I run workshops with leaders to find where AI could save the most time, and then I stay with the work until it is finished. That covers deciding what to build, designing how the AI helper talks and works, choosing the tools, testing, launching, and improving it once it is live. At every stage I hand over plans your IT team can build from.

Team training

I run practical training and team sessions based on Scrum and Agile, the ways of working that software teams use to deliver in small steps. I draw on fifteen years of delivering software.

Coaching for leaders

I coach leaders through the changes AI brings to how their team works from day to day.
