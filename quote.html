How it works

  1. 1

    Ask for a quote

    You tell me what you would like help with. This step is free.

  2. 2

    Agree where to start

    I email you a few short questions. We agree how AI can help you, which size fits, and how you would like to work. See prices. The emails and the quote are free.

  3. 3

    We build it your way

    You choose how we work. We can build it together on a video call, and I mentor you as we go. Or I can build it on my own and hand it over on a short call or with a written guide. I work with clients in any time zone, so we pick a time that suits you. You keep what we build.

  4. 4

    It keeps working

    Your solution keeps working for as long as it does its job. It tells you when it no longer fits and it is time for a follow-up. You can still make small changes yourself.
