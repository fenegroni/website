Good to know

Founding client rateMy launch rate is £50 an hour for sessions booked by 31 December 2026. I will use client feedback to refine the service, and new prices start in 2027. Any ongoing work is agreed with you before you commit.

How you payThe quote form never charges you. The £50 button takes you to a secure payment page. For a done-for-you option, I email you a payment link once you choose.

Prices and currenciesMy prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). If you choose another currency, the prices on this page are estimates at today's rate. You see the exact amount at checkout. Rates by Exchange Rate API.

After we finishYour hour includes a short written recap and seven days to email me questions. If something stops working later, email me. I tell you before any extra help costs money.

You are in chargeYou own the solution and can change it yourself. Where it fits, the solution checks itself and tells you when it may no longer suit you.

Running costsMy fee covers my time. Some solutions need subscriptions, API credits, a domain or better hardware. You pay for those directly, and I tell you before you book.

Skills you can trustA skill is a set of written instructions that teaches your AI one job. Skills from the internet can hide instructions you never asked for. I write or check every skill I give you, and I tell you where it came from.

Feedback, not a conditionI will ask how the session went and whether you still use what we built. A testimonial is optional, and I publish one only with your permission.

I am not a doctor or therapist. The solutions we build help with everyday friction. They do not diagnose or treat ADHD, and they do not replace medical care.
